Menu

Food

The "Splendid Splinter" Dog

Named after the legendary 1942 and 1947 triple crown winner, Teddy Williams. A massive, grilled footlong hot dog on a toasted brioche bun, loaded with beer cheese, crispy onion strings, and fresh jalapeños.

The Mick's 7-Bomb Burger

Named in honor of the 1956 triple crown winner and New York Yankees legend, Mickey Mantle. A half-pound double smash burger stacked with thick-cut bacon, smoked cheddar, lettuce, tomato, and house barbecue sauce.

Miggy's Nachos

A nod to MLB's most recent triple crown winner in 2012, Miguel Cabrera. A loaded mound of tortilla chips topped with braised pulled pork, black beans, queso, pico de gallo, and cilantro-lime crema.

Drinks

Frank Robinson's "1966" Old Fashioned

A timeless, powerful classic honoring the legendary 1966 AL Triple Crown winner (and the only player to win MVP in both leagues).

Ingredients: Bourbon, Angostura bitters, Demerara syrup, and an orange twist.

Carl Yastrzemski's "Boston Pop" Punch

A sweet, heavy-hitting tropical cocktail paying tribute to "Yaz" and his iconic 1967 Red Sox season.

Ingredients: Dark rum, spiced rum, pineapple juice, orange juice, grenadine, and a splash of dark cherry liqueur.

The "Grand Slam" Pitcher

A refreshingly upgraded beer-margarita designed for sharing at the table while watching the game.

Ingredients: Tequila Blanco, triple sec, fresh lime juice, agave nectar, topped with local craft IPA or crisp lager.
